Acute is the minutes you trained in the last 7 days. Chronic is the last 28 days divided by four, so both sides are per-week. The ratio is their quotient: under 0.8 is detraining, 0.8-1.3 is the range the research supports, and above 1.5 is where injury risk concentrates.
